🪔 Launching Diwali 2026

LabourVala

मजुरांचे हक्काचे व्यासपीठ · The trusted platform for workers

An ethical, Marathi-first marketplace for daily-wage work. Masons, carpenters, plumbers, electricians, painters, welders and drivers find work nearby, and hirers find reliable people. Starting in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

  • DigiLocker-verified identities. Aadhaar, PAN and police checks. We never store the full Aadhaar number.
  • Privacy by design. Applicants stay anonymous; numbers are revealed only after a hire is accepted.
  • Proof of arrival. A 4-digit code confirms the worker reached the site.
  • Two-way ratings. Every job builds a trusted work record.
  • GPS job discovery. Jobs within 10, 25 or 50 km with exact distance.
  • Projects for companies. Multi-site projects, daily arrivals and GST invoices.
  • English, मराठी, हिंदी. On the web, Android and iOS.
